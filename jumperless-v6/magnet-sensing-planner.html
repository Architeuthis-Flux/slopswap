Which sensor, which magnet, how many, how far. Everything below is recomputed from the controls: dipole field of the magnet, each sensor's saturation and noise, and the Cramér–Rao bound on probe position for a grid of sensors under the breadboard. Prices are LCSC at ~1k, observed 2026‑10‑18.

From the distance where the nearest sensor saturates to the distance where the signal drops to 10× its noise. The shaded band is below the breadboard surface, unreachable by the probe. In an array only the nearest sensor saturates; the others keep working, so the near edge is soft.

Best achievable position error vs height above the sensors

Cramér–Rao bound for one update at the chosen rate: 3‑D rms position error of a dipole with unknown position and moment (6 unknowns), using the whole grid, saturated sensors dropped, worst case over the probe's lateral position (above a sensor, between two, and in the middle of a cell) and over a 45° tilt. Any real filter sits above these lines; time averaging across updates moves them down by √N. The dashed 1‑axis line is ill‑conditioned close to the plane because a single axis can't separate tilt from offset; fixing the magnet strength or height would help it most.

Assumptions and sources

Magnet. Uniformly magnetized cylinder; the field curve uses the exact on‑axis formula, the position bound uses the dipole approximation with moment m = Br·V/µ0 (within ~10 % beyond two magnet diameters). Field falls as 1/z³ far out, so every sensor's distance window is only (saturation ÷ floor)^(1/3) wide, about 6–8×.

Noise. Datasheet rms noise at a reference rate, scaled by √(rate) for averaging, then added in quadrature with the residual ambient figure. MMC5603NJ 4 mG at BW=11 (1 kHz, 1.5 mG at 75 Hz); TMAG5273 147 µT XY at 1× (10 kSPS), 24 µT at 32×; TMAG3001 A2 106 µT / A1 95 µT at 1×; MLX90397 ~3 µT at 5 ms; TLV493D‑A1B6 ~100 µT with a 98 µT LSB (floored at LSB/√12); KTH5721 10–30 µT at high OSR (rate not stated, treated as 1 kHz); HAL403SO 2.4 mV rms into 40 mV/mT, one axis. The ambient figure is what calibration leaves behind: Earth's field and fixed hard iron subtract out, drift and moving metal do not.

Position bound. Fisher information from the dipole Jacobian over all unsaturated sensors, 6 unknowns (x, y, z, m⃗), inverted; reported as √(σx²+σy²+σz²). Constraints you actually have (known magnet strength, probe on the surface so z is known, temporal filtering) all improve on it.

Interference. Straight wire B = 2×10⁻⁷·I/r; a 2.54 mm supply/return pair ≈ 2×10⁻⁷·I·d/r². Unshielded drum‑core inductor estimated as a dipole with the core at ~0.15 T (≈ a 2×1 mm magnet): order 1 mT at 7 mm at rated current; shielded parts leak a few percent of that. Nickel terminations and magnetized steel leads/screws are the hard‑iron offsets that compass‑grade sensors see (µT to tens of µT, static). Rule of thumb from NXP: B[µT] = I[mA] / (5·r[mm]).

Datasheets: MMC5603NJ, TMAG5273, TMAG3001, MLX90397, TLV493D‑A1B6, NXP magnetometer placement.
