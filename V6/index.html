Jumperless V6

Working documents from the V6 design: an interactive unit-cost model and the decision notes behind it. Prices are LCSC unit prices on the dates each page states; everything is a draft until a board is built.

  • Jumperless V6 Cost Explorer

    Interactive BOM and unit-cost model: display, row isolation, current sense, expansion port, magnetometers, spring clips, cost cuts, board area, margin by price. Selections are remembered in your browser; datasheet links for every part at the bottom. Baseline of 31 October 2026.

  • ChonkBus: bus ends, interlock, row isolation, segments, BOM

    29–31 October 2026. The two identical 0.1 Ω buses, what each can connect to, the hardware/firmware interlock split, row isolation (fitted), latch bit map, BOM; §6: why 90 switches is the floor and the odd/even × top/bottom segment options (up to 4 or 8 nets, sheet b29_bussplit, PNG).

  • Current sense, always-on voltage sense, expansion port, cost cuts

    29–31 October 2026. Why ADCs on a shunt and the 470 Ω INA219 trick fall short; the floated-ground shunt amplifier; the housekeeping mux for ADC pins; M.2 / mini PCIe / SO-DIMM connector survey; the b38_expansion option sheet (PNG) with 39 routable pins; cost cuts and board area.
