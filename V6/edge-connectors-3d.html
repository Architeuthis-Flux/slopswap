Edge connectors in 3D

Each coplanar and right-angle connector from the connector options page, drawn to scale between the real WigLEDmag wig and a Proto barnacle plugged in at its top edge. The left view looks at the top face and the right view at the underside of the same assembly. The boards are the actual outlines and copper from the Barnacle_Board KiCad files; the plain strip added to each board is the area that connector would take. Housings are simplified blocks built from the catalogue dimensions, so trust the sizes and positions, not the fine detail.

      How to read it

      • Wig (below, nearer you): WigLEDmag, 78.7 × 55.0 mm without its tongue, 1.4 mm thick, 480 LEDs on top. Barnacle (above): the Proto barnacle, 1.6 mm thick. Both have had their real 45.47 mm tongues cut off and replaced by whatever that connector needs.
      • The plain strips are the dead area: the strip on the wig is as deep as the connector's legs or footprint, and the strip on the barnacle is the tongue it takes. A right-angle socket lifts the barnacle above the wig's top face by the height of its slot.
      • Fixed-size parts (PCIe, SFP, M.2) are shown in the number needed for 70 signals. Vertical-mount parts are left out.
      • Estimates: where a maker's page does not say how deep the card seats, or how high a right-angle slot sits, the model uses the same estimate as the calculator and the panel says so.
