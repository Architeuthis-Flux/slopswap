How the numbers are made

  • Coil strength. Moment = figure of merit × √(power in the coil). Figures of merit, A·m² per √W: keyfob 3D coil 0.0080 (from its 84 Ω and 72 mV per A/m), 2 × 30 rod 0.042, 3 × 45 rod 0.101, 4 × 60 rod 0.25 (a rod model that read a Coilcraft part a factor of two low). K in the tracker's mT·mm³ is 100 000 × the moment; the bench magnet is 1900.
  • Coil reach. Distance from the coil to the nearest MMC5603 where a 32-sample code still stands 5 times over the 0.4 µT noise, across the dipole's weak and strong directions. Combining all the sensors adds about a quarter.
  • Moving magnet. Moving the tracking magnet by a stroke d changes its field at a sensor a distance r away by up to 3 × d ÷ r of what it was, so the signal falls as 1 ÷ r⁴ where a coil's falls as 1 ÷ r³. Reach is where that change still stands 5 times over the noise across 32 samples; to the side of the stroke it is smaller, down to a third. The 30 mW drive, the stroke and the symbol rate are assumptions.
  • Usable energy. Capacitors: ½·C·(V_full² − V_low²). Cells: capacity × nominal voltage × 0.9; × 0.8 again through a boost converter, or × 3.3 ÷ the cell voltage through a regulator when the cell's full voltage is over the controller's limit. A capacitor is charged no higher than the controller allows.
  • Days of use. Usable energy ÷ (clicks a day × energy a click + a day of standby). Asleep is standby alone.
  • Dock time. Capacitors: through a resistor from 4.7 V, so the current tapers; the figure is R·C·ln of the two headrooms. LiPo: 1.3 × capacity ÷ current, at no more than 1C or the cell's own limit. Lithium titanate: 1.2 × capacity ÷ current. NiMH: 1.4 × capacity ÷ current, at no more than a tenth of the capacity, on a timer.
  • AAA and AA cells. Alkaline: 1200 mAh for an AAA (Energizer lists 1250) and 2800 mAh for an AA (about 2900 at 25 mA). NiMH: 800 and 2000 mAh, the eneloop figures, taken as losing 15 % in the first yearest. One click pulls about 60 mA from a single cell for an eighth of a second, which either kind gives easily.
  • Recharge circuit. No more than a tenth of the capacity, because at that rate a NiMH shows no clear sign of being full and tolerates the overrun a timer leaves; no trickle afterwards. Telling an alkaline from a NiMH by 1.5 V at rest and 1.6 V under charge is a proposal, not a measurement.
  • Haptic tick. The LRA at its rated 2.5 V rms and 317 mA through a bridge, 0.88 W, for the length set, once a click. Whether so short a pulse is felt, and what the moving magnet does to the tracker, are not known.
  • Body width. The widest part across the probe, the controller's package and its board included, plus a 1 mm wall each side. The dock strip is 7.62 mm wide and the probe drawn in the outline tool is 3.6 mm.
