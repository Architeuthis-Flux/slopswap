California things to know while you design

More detail here because it drives the design. APCCO owns all of it and gives the exact numbers.

Refrigerant: why not Freon

  • California (CARB), since 2022: a new system over 50 lb of charge in a new facility needs a refrigerant with GWP under 150. R-404A is about 3,900, R-507 about 4,000, R-448A/R-449A about 1,390, even R-32 is 675. Under 150 leaves ammonia (0), CO₂ (1), or A2L blends like R-454C/R-455A (about 148), which we don't want at this size.
  • Federal (EPA): process refrigeration with evaporators between −30 and −50 °C can't use GWP 700 or more from 1 January 2028. Our later freezers would land after that; we won't build on a refrigerant we can't repeat.
  • Over 50 lb of any HFC also means California's refrigerant management program: registration, leak inspections, reporting. Ammonia and CO₂ are outside it.
  • So: ammonia, as planned. CO₂ also passes.

Ammonia rules

  • Design to ANSI/IIAR 2-2021; install, start up and inspect to IIAR 4, 5 and 6; plus the California Mechanical Code. APCCO works to these daily.
  • On the drawings: high-side design pressure (typically 250 psig / 17.2 bar; plates and hot-gas piping carry it; APCCO confirms); relief valves on every vessel, piped to a safe point outdoors; a labelled emergency shutoff; pipe and valve labels (refrigerant, pressure level, direction); ammonia detectors in the freezer room and at the package, wired to alarm and shut down.
  • Ammonia equipment inside a room makes it a "machinery room" (ventilation, detection, door controls, restricted access, no open flames). The outdoor package avoids most of that. Tell us whether your container is built as a machinery room (fans, detectors, door switches) or as open-air equipment.
  • Paperwork by charge: any ammonia means a hazardous-materials plan with the county; 500 lb or more adds CalARP (risk management plan, hazard review, inspections); 10,000 lb adds federal PSM/RMP, which we'll never reach. Lowest charge, least paperwork, and we need the number early.
  • Leave room for an eyewash and shower at the package.

Vessels and piping

  • Vessels: ASME Section VIII Div. 1, U-stamp, National Board number. Cal/OSHA permits each ammonia vessel and wants weld radiography; APCCO says which and how much.
  • Piping: ASME B31.5; welders to ASME IX; material certificates; pressure-test records for anything shipped pre-piped.
  • No copper or brass in the ammonia circuit; hose data sheets; plate pressure rating and test pressure on paper.

Electrical

  • California Electrical Code is the NEC plus California additions. Every item listed by a US lab (UL, ETL or CSA); the inspector reads the labels. Unlisted gear means a field evaluation: slow, expensive, sometimes fails.
  • Panels to UL 508A with the SCCR on the label; NEMA 4X in washdown, 3R or 4 outdoors; NFPA 79 wiring and colours; English labels and drawings.
  • A lockable disconnect within sight of each motor, and a main disconnect per freezer and per skid.
  • Motors at DOE efficiency (NEMA Premium / IE3) with the efficiency on the nameplate; VFDs and soft starters listed too.

Food safety

  • FDA: seafood HACCP for the squid, animal-food rules for the paste. Cleanable, corrosion-resistant contact surfaces; no flaking galvanizing or paint over product; drains and slopes; NSF H1 lubricants where product could be touched.

Worker safety

  • Cal/OSHA: guarded pinch points, E-stops, lockout points for electrical and hydraulic energy, English warning labels. Any loading platform needs guardrails, toe boards and proper stairs; include it in the design.

Noise

  • Limits at the property line, lower at night, and we run 20 hours a day. Give condenser and compressor noise (dB(A) at 10 m) with the proposal.

Permits, and who files what

  • The refrigeration package is skid-mounted portable equipment and is handled as equipment, not construction. Keep it that way: lifting points, one electrical connection, flanged pipe connections, nothing cast into the ground.
  • Work on the building (feeder to the skid, freezer room, wall penetrations) goes through normal permits: APCCO, on stamped drawings.
  • Unaffected by how the package is mounted: the refrigerant rules (anything in one place over 12 months is stationary to CARB and EPA), the county's hazardous-materials plan and CalARP (quantity on site), Cal/OSHA vessel permits and ASME stamps, electrical listing, food safety, worker safety, noise.
  • County (CUPA) and CalARP: Del Mar with APCCO. Cal/OSHA vessel permits: APCCO. PG&E service and food-plant registration: Del Mar. The county and PG&E paperwork can take longer than manufacturing, so drawings first.

Paperwork you'll be asked for. 设计时要知道的加州规定

这一节细一些，因为它直接影响设计。都以 APCCO 为准，具体数值由他们给。

制冷剂：为什么不能用氟利昂

  • 加州（CARB）2022 年起：新建设施里充注量超过 50 磅的新系统，制冷剂 GWP 须低于 150。R-404A 约 3,900，R-507 约 4,000，R-448A/R-449A 约 1,390，R-32 也有 675。低于 150 的只有氨（0）、CO₂（1），或 R-454C/R-455A 这类 A2L 混合制冷剂（约 148），这个规模我们不想用。
  • 联邦（EPA）：蒸发温度 −30 到 −50 °C 的工艺制冷，2028 年 1 月 1 日起不得用 GWP 700 以上的制冷剂。之后加的冻结机正好落在那之后；我们不会建在一种没法复制的制冷剂上。
  • 超过 50 磅氢氟烃还要进加州的制冷剂管理计划：登记、泄漏检查、报告。氨和 CO₂ 不在其内。
  • 所以：按计划用氨。CO₂ 也能过关。

氨的规定

  • 设计按 ANSI/IIAR 2-2021；安装、开机、检验按 IIAR 4、5、6；加《加州机械规范》。APCCO 天天按这些干活。
  • 落到图纸上：高压侧设计压力（通常 250 psig / 17.2 bar，冻结板和热氨管路也要承受，APCCO 确认）；每个容器有安全阀，排到室外安全点；有标识的紧急切断阀；管道和阀门标识（制冷剂、压力等级、流向）；冻结机车间和机组处有氨探测器，联动报警和停机。
  • 氨设备放在房间里，那个房间就成了"机房"（通风、探测、门口控制、限制进入、禁止明火）。室外机组避开大部分。请说明你们的集装箱是按机房做的（风机、探测器、门开关）还是按露天设备。
  • 按充注量分的手续：有氨就要向县里报危险品经营计划；500 磅以上加 CalARP（风险管理计划、危害分析、检查）；10,000 磅加联邦 PSM/RMP，我们到不了。充注量最低、手续最少，数字要早给。
  • 机组旁留洗眼器和冲淋的位置。

容器和管道

  • 容器：ASME 第 VIII 卷第 1 册，U 钢印，National Board 编号。Cal/OSHA 给每个氨容器发许可证并要求焊缝拍片；哪些、多少，APCCO 说。
  • 管道：ASME B31.5；焊工按 ASME 第 IX 卷；材质证明；预装管路附压力试验记录。
  • 氨回路不用铜和黄铜；软管附数据表；冻结板的压力等级和试验压力要有书面文件。

电气

  • 《加州电气规范》就是 NEC 加加州补充。每件设备都要有美国机构认证（UL、ETL 或 CSA），检查员看标签。没认证的要现场评估：慢、贵、可能不过。
  • 控制柜按 UL 508A，标签上标 SCCR；冲洗区 NEMA 4X，室外 3R 或 4；接线和线色按 NFPA 79；标识和图纸用英文。
  • 每台电机视线内有可上锁的隔离开关，每台冻结机和每个撬块各一个总开关。
  • 电机满足美国能源部能效（NEMA Premium / IE3），铭牌标能效；变频器和软启动器也要认证。

食品安全

  • FDA：鱿鱼按水产品 HACCP，鱼糜按动物食品规定。接触面易清洗、耐腐蚀；产品上方不能有会掉皮的镀锌或油漆；有排水和坡度；可能碰到产品的地方用 NSF H1 润滑油。

人员安全

  • Cal/OSHA：夹点防护、急停、电气和液压能量的上锁点、英文警示。装料平台要有护栏、踢脚板和正规楼梯，请做进设计里。

噪音

  • 地界处有限值，夜间更低，我们每天运行 20 小时。请随报价给出冷凝器和压缩机噪音（10 m 处 dB(A)）。

许可证，谁办什么

  • 制冷机组是撬装的可移动设备，按设备而不是按建筑处理。请保持这个属性：吊点、一个电源接口、法兰管道接口，不要有浇在地里的东西。
  • 厂房上的工作（到撬块的馈线、冻结机车间、穿墙）走正常许可证：APCCO，凭盖章图纸办。
  • 不受安装方式影响的：制冷剂规定（在一个地方放超过 12 个月，CARB 和 EPA 都算固定式）、县里的危险品经营计划和 CalARP（看现场氨量）、Cal/OSHA 容器许可证和 ASME 钢印、电气认证、食品安全、人员安全、噪音。
  • 县（CUPA）和 CalARP：Del Mar 和 APCCO。Cal/OSHA 容器许可证：APCCO。PG&E 用电和食品厂登记：Del Mar。县里和 PG&E 的手续可能比造机器还慢，所以图纸先行。

你们会被要求提供的文件。可盖章的图纸（布置图、P&ID、单线图、原理图）；设计压力和安全阀选型计算；每个容器的 ASME U-1/U-1A 报告和 National Board 编号；材质证明和焊工资质；压力试验记录；每个控制柜的 UL 508A 标签和 SCCR 计算；电机能效铭牌；重量和吊点；氨充注量计算；英文操作维护手册。哪项提供不了现在就说，APCCO 会想办法。
